Please create 3 images, aspect ratio 3:2, at the highest resolution available (ideally 2400x1600 or larger each). Use the attached reference sprites for all characters, and match the pixel style of the attached cover_index and scene_s5 images.

COMMON STYLE (apply to every image):
16-bit SNES-era RPG pixel art rendered at high resolution with clean, sharp, crisp pixel edges (no blur, no JPEG artifacts, no upscaling smear), limited palette, dithering allowed, no anti-aliased blur, no smooth anime shading, no 3D render. Bright daytime research lab: white walls and white floor tiles dominate the background, accents in soft teal, orange, navy and red. Characters must match the attached reference sprites exactly (same faces, hair, outfits), drawn as small chibi pixel sprites about 48–64 px tall in the scene:
- Professor: spiky black hair with one white streak, teal rectangular glasses, white lab coat over black vest and tie.
- Assistant Haru: long pink hair with a blue streak, two hair buns, lavender hoodie under a white lab coat. Today she is nervous (small sweat drop) unless the scene says otherwise.
- Fruit-fly researcher: navy round helmet with big red compound eyes and blue tubes, black insect body in a white lab coat.
- Abacus robot: beige boxy CRT-monitor head with a green pixel smile and a small antenna, wooden abacus belly, gray arms, tank treads.
- Intern Sia: long straight black hair, black maid-style lab dress with white frilled apron and gray bow.
- Intern Moa: long light-brown hair, white maid headband, mustard maid-style dress with white apron and red plaid bow.
- Audience X1: small shadow-black chibi with a long black ponytail and a big pink bow, white dot eyes, pink cheeks.
- Audience X2: small shadow-black chibi with a dark beret and round teal glasses.
- Audience X3: small shadow-black chibi in a cat-ear hood with two gold-tipped hoodie strings.
Do NOT draw any text, letters, numbers or symbols with writing. Number cards and boards are drawn as blank tiles or dots only.
Do NOT draw any company logo, brand mark or real product. The AI helpers in IMAGE 2 are generic, original tiny robots.

Morning in the white lab, sunlight through the window. The abacus robot bursts in through the door on its tank treads, holding up a tablet with a big glowing blank screen, eyes wide with excitement, little motion lines. The fruit-fly researcher stands with arms crossed and leans in, poking the wooden abacus on the robot's belly with one finger, smirking and teasing. Sia and Moa look confused with question-mark-shaped sweat drops (no actual symbols, just puzzled faces). The professor sips coffee in the background, amused. Haru peeks from behind the professor, already a little nervous. X1, X2, X3 peek from behind a desk.

Inside a bright workshop: one human mathematician (a generic adult in a sweater with a notebook, NOT one of the lab characters) works at a long desk together with about eight tiny friendly helper robots of the same simple original design (small round white bodies, single soft orange light). The helpers pass blank paper sheets along like a relay and stack them into a tall glowing tower of proof pages. Next to the tower stands a huge teal machine like a giant checking press: pages go in one at a time and each comes out with a big green check-mark glow (a check shape only, no letters). The abacus robot watches with its abacus, clicking beads to help with calculations; the fruit-fly researcher inspects one page with a magnifier; Haru explains to Sia and Moa pointing at the machine. X1, X2, X3 watch amazed.

End of the day, warm light in the white lab.
