# 대화 3 — 장면 그림 1~4단계

[젠스파크에 붙이는 순서]
1. refs 폴더의 캐릭터 기본 그림 10장(ref_*.png)을 먼저 올린다.
2. cover_index.png를 "그림체 참고"로 함께 올린다.
3. 아래 요청문을 그대로 붙인다. 한 대화에서 아래 장수만 만든다.
4. 받은 그림을 저장 이름대로 저장한다.

---

Please create 4 images, aspect ratio 3:2, about 1200x800 each. Use the attached reference sprites for all characters.

COMMON STYLE (apply to every image):
16-bit SNES-era RPG pixel art, crisp pixel edges, limited palette, dithering allowed, no anti-aliased blur, no smooth anime shading, no 3D render. Bright daytime research lab: white walls and white floor tiles dominate the background, accents in soft teal, orange, navy and red. Characters must match the attached reference sprites exactly (same faces, hair, outfits), drawn as small chibi pixel sprites about 48–64 px tall in the scene:
- Professor: spiky black hair with one white streak, teal rectangular glasses, white lab coat over black vest and tie.
- Assistant Haru: long pink hair with a blue streak, two hair buns, lavender hoodie under a white lab coat. Today she is nervous (small sweat drop) unless the scene says otherwise.
- Fruit-fly researcher: navy round helmet with big red compound eyes and blue tubes, black insect body in a white lab coat.
- Abacus robot: beige boxy CRT-monitor head with a green pixel smile and a small antenna, wooden abacus belly, gray arms, tank treads.
- Intern Sia: long straight black hair, black maid-style lab dress with white frilled apron and gray bow.
- Intern Moa: long light-brown hair, white maid headband, mustard maid-style dress with white apron and red plaid bow.
- Audience X1: small shadow-black chibi with a long black ponytail and a big pink bow, white dot eyes, pink cheeks.
- Audience X2: small shadow-black chibi with a dark beret and round teal glasses.
- Audience X3: small shadow-black chibi in a cat-ear hood with two gold-tipped hoodie strings.
Do NOT draw any text, letters, numbers or symbols with writing. Number cards and boards are drawn as blank tiles or dots only.

IMAGE 1 — save as scene_s1.png  (1단계: 소수는 숫자의 레고 블록)
A large 10-by-10 grid board of blank tiles on the lab wall. The abacus robot slides abacus beads and many tiles turn gray, leaving scattered tiles glowing orange. Next to it, pixel toy blocks: a big block splitting into two small blocks and one more small block. Haru explains with a pointer (slightly nervous), Sia points at a glowing tile excitedly.

IMAGE 2 — save as scene_s2.png  (2단계: 소수는 어디에 쓰이나)
Left: two small glowing orange blocks snapping together into one big padlock instantly with a sparkle. Right: the abacus robot struggling to pry the big padlock apart, sweating, with a long trail of discarded keys behind it. Bottom right: a cabinet of drawers where items are spread evenly, one in each drawer. Moa tilts her head thoughtfully, Haru gestures between the padlock and the drawers. A tiny cicada sits on top of the drawer cabinet.

IMAGE 3 — save as scene_s3.png  (3단계: 뒤로 갈수록 드물어진다)
A long road stretching to the right with small bus-stop signs: dense at the start, then farther and farther apart toward the horizon, but never ending. Sia and Moa stand at the start looking toward the horizon; the abacus robot counts on its abacus. Haru, calmer now, holds a small flag at the first stop.

IMAGE 4 — save as scene_s4.png  (4단계: 어림으로 맞혀 보기)
A whiteboard with two lines climbing almost on top of each other: a solid navy stepped line and a dashed teal smooth line. Haru looks relieved and points at how close they are; Sia holds a weather-forecast style card with a sun icon (no text); Moa compares with a magnifying glass. X1 in the corner gives a small cheer.
